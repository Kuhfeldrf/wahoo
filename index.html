Fin checklist

Every fin a wahoo has is on the model. Tap a fin to light it up in orange on the 3D view.

    Build sheet

    Overall length
    308 mm (12.1 in)
    Height, fin to fin
    72 mm
    Each printed half
    308 × 72 × 14 mm, flat side down
    Supports
    none needed
    Est. plastic
    ≈ 63 g PLA · ≈ $10 at 16¢/g
    Buoyancy
    displaces ≈ 131 g of water
    Ballast pocket
    54 × 6 × 6.5 mm · up to ≈ 20 g lead
    Tow channel
    3.5 mm, nose → belly exit
    Eye recess
    8 mm stick-on lure eyes
    Alignment
    4 holes for 1.75 mm filament pins
    1. Drop lead into the ballast pocket and filament pins into the holes.
    2. Run the leader or cable through the tow channel before closing.
    3. Glue the halves with marine epoxy, clamp, then sand the seam.
    4. Paint: dark cobalt back, silver sides, blue vertical bars, dark fins.
    5. Press in the eyes and finish with 2–3 coats of clear epoxy.
